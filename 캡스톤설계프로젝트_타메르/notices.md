### 원문

Dear Students,

Welcome to *Capstone Design* for the Fall 2026 semester.

This is a **project-based, team-oriented course** where you will design and implement a complete system from idea to final demonstration.

The objective is to apply your knowledge to develop a practical software or hardware solution in a structured and professional manner.

We will meet **offline this Friday** to officially start the course, discuss project directions, and answer any questions you may have.

#### **Course Structure:**

- This is a **team project course** -- there are **no exams**.
- We will form **teams of 4 students**.
- I encourage you to start thinking about your **project interests and ideas** (software or hardware).
- During our first meeting, you will share your interests so we can match students with similar goals and build effective teams.

I look forward to meeting you this Friday and seeing your ideas.

Best regards,
Prof. Tamer
